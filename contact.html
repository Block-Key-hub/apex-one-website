Common Questions

Quick Answers

Owner

How do I get started with Apex One?

Contact us for a free rental analysis. We'll evaluate your property, discuss our services, and walk you through our management agreement. Most owners are onboarded within 5–7 business days.

Owner

How do you handle maintenance requests?

All maintenance requests go through our online portal. We coordinate with licensed contractors, get owner approval for larger repairs, and handle everything from routine fixes to major rehabilitations.

Tenant

How do I apply for a rental?

Browse our available units and click Apply Now on any listing. Our application takes about 10 minutes and we typically respond within 1–2 business days.

Tenant

How do I submit a maintenance request?

Log into the Tenant Portal and submit a request with photos if possible. We prioritize urgent requests and aim to have all issues addressed within 48 hours.
