Why Metro Detroit Owners Choose Us

Small team, serious results. Every property we manage gets the same level of care we'd give our own.

  • Full TransUnion Screening — At No Cost to You

    Every applicant is screened for credit, criminal history, and eviction records. Applicants pay their own screening fees, so it costs you nothing.

  • Listings Syndicated to Zillow, Trulia, HotPads & Realtor.com

    Your vacancy goes live across the top rental platforms automatically, so you get qualified applicants faster and vacancies stay short.

  • Automated Late Fee Enforcement

    Late fees are applied and tracked automatically — no awkward conversations, no manual follow-up, no missed collections.

  • Maintenance Tracked with Full Photo & Video Trail

    Every request is logged from submission to completion with photos and video. You always know what was done, when, and why.

  • Detailed Monthly Financial Statements

    Clear income and expense reports delivered every month, plus consistent, predictable disbursements so you always know what to expect.

  • Local Market Expertise

    Deep roots in Metro Detroit neighborhoods — we know the market, the tenants, and what it takes to keep your property performing.
