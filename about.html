Who We Are

Built for Detroit.
Built for You.

Apex One Property Management was founded with a simple belief — that managing real estate should be straightforward, transparent, and professional for everyone involved. We serve Metro Detroit property owners and tenants with the same standard of care we use on our own homes and investments.


From tenant screening to maintenance coordination, rent collection to financial reporting, we handle every detail so you don't have to. Our team brings deep local market knowledge and hands-on real estate experience to every property we manage — giving owners confidence and tenants a place they're proud to call home.
