Our Services

Everything Under One Roof

Tenant Screening & Placement

Comprehensive background checks including credit, criminal, eviction history, employment verification, and rental references. We only place tenants we'd be comfortable with in our own properties.

Rent Collection

Online rent collection through our tenant portal with autopay, late fee automation, and payment reminders. Owners receive disbursements within 5–7 business days of collection.

Maintenance Coordination

24/7 maintenance request handling via our online portal. We coordinate with licensed, vetted contractors and get owner approval for repairs above agreed thresholds.

Financial Reporting

Detailed monthly financial statements including income, expenses, and net cash flow. Full transparency into every dollar in and out of your property.

Leasing & Lease Renewals

Professional lease preparation, e-signature, and secure document storage. We proactively reach out to tenants 60–90 days before expiration to minimize vacancy.

Compliance & Legal

We stay current on Michigan landlord-tenant law, local rental licensing requirements, fair housing regulations, and Section 8 compliance so you don't have to.
