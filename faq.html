Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about renting, owning, and working with Apex One

For Owners

Property Owner Questions

How do I get started with Apex One?

Contact us for a free rental analysis. We evaluate your property, discuss our full-service management offering, and walk you through our management agreement. Most owners are fully onboarded within 5–7 business days.

What does your management fee cover?

Our management fee covers tenant screening and placement, rent collection, maintenance coordination, monthly financial reporting, lease administration, and legal compliance. There are no hidden fees — everything is disclosed upfront.

How do you screen tenants?

We run comprehensive background checks including credit history, criminal background, eviction history, employment verification, and rental references. We only place tenants we'd be comfortable with in our own properties.

How is rent collected and distributed?

Rent is collected through our online portal. After deducting management fees and any approved expenses, net proceeds are disbursed to owners via direct deposit typically within 5–7 business days of collection.

What happens if a tenant doesn't pay rent?

We take the stress out of difficult tenant situations. We issue late notices, serve pay or quit notices, and document everything thoroughly. If legal action becomes necessary, we'll connect you with a trusted eviction attorney and stay involved so you're never navigating it alone.

How do you handle maintenance and repairs?

All maintenance requests go through our portal. You set the spending limit upfront — we handle anything below it immediately, and nothing above it moves forward without your direct approval. No surprise invoices, no decisions made without you. We coordinate only with licensed, vetted contractors from routine fixes to major repairs.

Can I see financial reports for my property?

Yes. Owners receive detailed monthly financial statements including income, expenses, and net cash flow. Our owner portal gives you 24/7 access to all reports and property documents.

Do you handle Section 8 / Housing Choice Voucher tenants?

Yes. We are experienced with HUD Housing Choice Voucher program requirements and can help qualify your property for Section 8, which often provides higher guaranteed rents and reduced vacancy risk.

For Tenants

Tenant Questions

How do I apply for a rental?

Browse our available units at apexonepm.com/rentals and click Apply Now on any listing. Our application takes about 10 minutes. We typically respond within 1–2 business days.

What do I need to qualify?

We look for verifiable income of at least 3x the monthly rent, a credit score of 580 or above, no prior evictions within the past 5 years, and a review of full credit and background history. Each application is evaluated individually and holistically — we consider the full picture, not just one factor.

How much is the security deposit?

Security deposits are typically equal to one month's rent. The exact amount will be stated clearly in your lease agreement before you sign.

How do I pay rent?

Rent is paid online through our Tenant Portal. You can set up automatic payments so you never miss a due date. We accept ACH bank transfers and most major debit cards.

How do I submit a maintenance request?

Log into the Tenant Portal and submit a maintenance request with a description and photos if possible. We prioritize urgent issues and aim to address all requests within 48 hours.

What counts as an emergency maintenance issue?

Emergencies include no heat in winter, flooding, gas leaks, no hot water, electrical hazards, and security issues like broken locks. Call our emergency line immediately for these — do not wait for portal response.

Can I renew my lease?

We reach out to tenants 60–90 days before lease expiration to discuss renewal options. We value good tenants and work to make renewal easy and straightforward.

What is your pet policy?

Pet policies vary by property. Some properties allow cats and/or small dogs with a pet deposit. Check the individual listing for pet policy details or contact us to ask about a specific property.
